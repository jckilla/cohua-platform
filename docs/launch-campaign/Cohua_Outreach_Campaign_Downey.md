## 5. 50% Off Offer Structure

### Recommended Framing: "Charter Partner Program"

| Element | Details |
|---------|--------|
| Program Name | Cohua Charter Partner Program |
| Discount | 50% off standard rates for the entire portfolio |
| Lock-In Period | 12 months at Charter pricing |
| Who Qualifies | Property management companies onboarding during the Downey market launch (limited to first 5 partners) |
| What Happens After 12 Months | Rate increases to standard pricing, but Charter Partners receive a permanent 15% loyalty discount |
| Urgency Mechanism | Limited to 5 Charter Partner slots per market |

### Why This Framing Works

**"Charter Partner" vs. "Discount":** The word "charter" implies founding membership and exclusivity, not desperation.

**12-Month Lock-In:** Long enough to feel substantial but short enough to seem like a real window.

**5 Partner Slots:** A specific, small number creates real scarcity.

**Post-Charter Loyalty Discount:** Softens the "what happens after?" objection with a permanent 15% discount.

### Language to Use in Outreach

| Instead Of | Say This |
|-----------|----------|
| "50% discount" | "Charter Partner pricing at 50% off" |
| "Limited time offer" | "We have 5 Charter Partner slots remaining in Downey" |
| "Special deal" | "Early-adopter rate locked in for 12 months" |
| "Act now" | "We're filling our launch partner positions and wanted to reach out to you first" |
| "Cheap" / "Affordable" | "Half the standard rate for your entire portfolio" |

### Handling Objections About Pricing

**"Why so cheap?":** "We're not discounting because the product isn't worth full price --- we're investing in our launch partners. We want 5 property management companies in Downey who help us prove the model. In exchange, you get the best rate we'll ever offer."

**"What happens after 12 months?":** "You'll step up to our standard rate, but Charter Partners keep a permanent 15% loyalty discount. And by then, your tenants will have 12 months of AR ad performance data showing the ROI."

**"Can I get a longer lock-in?":** "The 12-month Charter rate is fixed, but we're open to discussing multi-year agreements at your renewal. Partners who onboard early will always get the most favorable terms."
