**Executive Summary**

This plan charts Cohua's path from 0 to 1,000 clients in 40 days (March
21 -- April 30, 2026). The core strategy leverages property management
companies as force multipliers: a single PM company managing 50--200
commercial tenants can convert an entire portfolio into Cohua clients in
one deal.

The FIFA World Cup (June 2026 at SoFi Stadium) creates unprecedented
urgency for businesses in the Greater LA area to invest in
next-generation advertising. Cohua's GPS-anchored AR ads on Ray-Ban Meta
glasses position businesses to capture foot traffic from millions of
visitors.

**Key Strategic Pillars**

-   **Property Management Multiplier:** One PM deal = dozens to hundreds
    of clients. Target 30--35 PM companies to reach 1,000.

-   **FIFA Urgency:** Businesses near SoFi Stadium need to act now to be
    visible during the World Cup.

-   **Early-Adopter Pricing:** 50% discount creates urgency and lowers
    the barrier for first movers.

-   **Multi-Market Expansion:** Start in Downey/Inglewood, then expand
    to NYC or SF by Week 3.

-   **Outreach Volume:** 2,900+ emails, 1,080+ calls, 500+ LinkedIn
    touches over 40 days.

**Risk Mitigation & Contingencies**

Reaching 1,000 clients in 40 days is aggressive. Below are the primary
risks and corresponding contingency plans.

**Risk: PM Companies Close Slower Than Expected**
Mitigation: Run parallel direct-to-business outreach in high-density commercial areas. Target 50--100 direct deals as a buffer.

**Risk: Demo Not Ready by Week 1**
Mitigation: Use the 90-second video demo for initial outreach while the live demo is being polished.

**Risk: Second Market Doesn't Convert**
Mitigation: Double down on LA. The Greater LA market has enough PM companies to reach 1,000 clients without a second city.

**Risk: Platform Can't Scale to 1,000**
Mitigation: CTO begins load testing at Week 3 (300 clients). Onboarding is staggered by PM company.

**Risk: SDR Hires Underperform**
Mitigation: CEO and COO maintain their own outreach cadence throughout. SDRs are additive, not a replacement.
